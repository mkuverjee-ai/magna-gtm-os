How a test runs

  1. Open the GTM OS on your laptop with gtm-work and load the play with /play <name>.
  2. Build the list and copy there; /launch creates the campaign in Instantly or lemlist as a draft.
  3. You read it and press go. Results come back here through the play's numbers.
  4. When a test clears its bar, it becomes a proposal for the machine.
