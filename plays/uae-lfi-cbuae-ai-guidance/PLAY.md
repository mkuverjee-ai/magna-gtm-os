## Why this segment, why now

In February 2026 the Central Bank of the UAE issued its Guidance Note on the Consumer Protection
and Responsible Adoption and Use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning by Licensed
Financial Institutions. It applies to all LFIs the CBUAE supervises and reaches into governance,
model risk management, outsourcing and operational resilience. It is not legally binding, but
"institutions should expect the Guidance Note to form part of supervisory dialogue and regulatory
assessments going forward", and "regulatory responsibility cannot be outsourced".
Sources: Hadef & Partners briefing (read 12 Nov 2026), GoTrust article of 23 Apr 2026. See
`research/pqs/segment.md`.

Changed from the first draft (Saudi banks · SAMA AI rule): no SAMA AI-specific rule for banks
could be verified on 12 Nov 2026, so a Saudi version would rest on an unverified trigger.

## Success bar

- T1 is read on positive replies per arm. 60 sends per arm can show a direction only; the machine's
  evidence bar for calling a winner is 250 sends per arm.
- Stop early (dead-end rule): 0 positive replies in 100 sends across the play.
- Scale: version C at least matches A on positive replies with bounce and unsubscribes no worse;
  then propose C to the machine as a third draft option, never as a replacement on this evidence.

## Test design (T1)

1. One list of about 120 contacts from this segment, deduped against every live machine campaign.
2. Split by alternating rows: half get version A (the machine's current Touch 1 and follow-ups,
   written by the machine's copywriter for each lead), half get version C (`campaigns/version-c.md`).
3. Same sender pool, same days, same send windows, same follow-up timing (day 0, 4, 9, 16).
4. Minal reads and approves both versions for every lead before anything is loaded.
