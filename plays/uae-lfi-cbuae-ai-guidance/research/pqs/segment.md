# Segment: UAE LFIs under the CBUAE AI guidance note

Status: **belief-led**. Magna has no closed-won bank customer to backtest against, and none of the
machine's 10 logged replies so far came from a cold bank lead (every positive reply came from LEAP
2026 event follow-ups). Treat this as a hypothesis until T1 and T3 have data.

## Anatomy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | A UAE bank or insurer is already using AI with customers, and its regulator has just written down what it expects. |
| **Tension** | H1: AI is spreading across chatbots, scoring, fraud and marketing tools, much of it from vendors. H2: the CBUAE says responsibility for that AI cannot be outsourced, and control functions must be able to challenge it. |
| **Amplifier** | The guidance note (February 2026) is expected to "form part of supervisory dialogue and regulatory assessments going forward". |
| **Existential data point** | Count of AI systems in customer-facing use with no named owner or inventory entry. Unknown from outside; the first conversation finds it. |
| **Who feels it** | Chief Risk Officer, Head of Model Risk, Chief Compliance Officer, CISO, Chief Data Officer or Head of AI. CRO and CCO can usually sponsor; CISO and CDO often need the CRO's backing. |
