A plain, classic introduction: who I am, what Magna AI is, and one clear ask (an assessment or a
meeting). Deliberately ignores two machine style rules ("I hope you are well" and leading with the
company) so the test can show whether the simple approach beats the research-led ones.

Salutation: "{honorific} {last_name}" ONLY when the honorific (Mr. or Ms.) is confirmed for that person;
Minal sets it at approval. Never guessed from the name. Otherwise "{first_name} {last_name}".

Merge fields: {salutation}, {first_name}, {company}. Status: draft for Minal's review. Nothing is sent.
