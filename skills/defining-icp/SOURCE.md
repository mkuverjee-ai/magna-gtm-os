## How it fits the Magna GTM OS

- `company/context.md` is still the source of truth for Magna's ICP; this skill is a way of thinking about it, not a replacement.
- Use it to challenge or narrow a segment, then hand over to the existing skills: `pqs-builder` (pain-qualified segments), `icp-matrix` and `icp-tam-mapper` (the matrix and market size), `tam-scoring` (scoring and tiers).
- Its examples are mostly US B2B SaaS. Translate them to Magna's buyers (governments, regulated enterprises, data-centre projects in the Gulf, Africa and Asia) before acting on them.
- Its "comically narrow beachhead" advice applies per play, not to the company ICP as a whole.
