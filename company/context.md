## Message rules

**Voice:** a smart commercial professional who has done the homework. Peer to peer, short, specific.

**Register by audience:**
- CEO, minister, board, chairman, group MD: "Hi [First name]," and a senior, concise register.
- C-level and VPs below CEO: conversational, first name, peer to peer.
- Middle East: respectful, concise, relationship-oriented, never patronising.
- Africa: relationship first, credibility before casualness, no slang.

**Structure (touch 1):** a research hook (one dated, verifiable signal from the company) → the business problem it implies → how Magna helps (one product at most) → one low-friction ask. 80-150 words (up to about 250 for a full four-layer email). Follow-ups and LinkedIn: insight-led, observation → implication → question. Short paragraphs, a blank line between each. Emails go out as HTML with Minal's signature added automatically.

**Follow-ups:** never "just following up"; each touch adds a new reason to reply. The last follow-up uses the proven close (see What has worked).

**Subject lines:** short, specific, natural, max 8 words, lowercase-leaning is fine (e.g. "AI governance at {Company}"). Never "free", "$", a price or any money amount, and never the offer.

**CTA:** one per message. Default: a 20-minute conversation. Use phrasings like "Would you be open to a quick conversation?", "Would it be useful to compare notes?", "Would 20 minutes be worthwhile?" and vary them. Avoid "Book a demo", "Schedule a discovery call" and "Click here to book".

**Prefer:** sovereignty, data residency, production-ready, governance, explainability, automation, operational efficiency, resilience, continuity, compliance. Cybersecurity is a business and operational issue, never fear-based.

**Avoid:** synergies, unlock unprecedented value, best-in-class, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionise, "I hope this message finds you well", "I am reaching out to introduce myself", generic "AI is transforming everything".

**NEVER:**
1. Name another company as a peer or comparison. The only company names allowed in outbound text are the recipient's and Trend Micro, Wistron and NVIDIA (as parents and partner).
2. Name any client, partner, target, investor, reference or security vendor, including everything on the deck's ecosystem slide. Never mention Westcon, CleanStart, Veptr, TxOne, VicOne, TrendAI or TrendLife.
3. Quote customer results or testimonials (none are approved).
4. Fake urgency or sound desperate.
5. Over-explain the product, list features or write long company descriptions.
6. Claim a problem the prospect has without evidence.
7. Apply Western sales norms without local adjustment.

If a prospect asks who Magna has done this for, hand the thread to Minal; don't answer.

**ALWAYS:** connect every message to a business hypothesis, prove the research with one specific, verifiable signal, use one CTA, and make the prospect the centre.

**Approval:** nothing is sent without Minal's approval.

## What has worked so far

- **Proven close (Minal's emails that got positive replies, e.g. a CTO forwarded the thread to his team):** the last follow-up opens "Last note from me.", adds one specific in-market point they haven't heard yet, restates the no-commitment first step, then closes: "If the timing's wrong, tell me when to circle back and I'll do exactly that. If it's not a fit at all, reply "not now" and I'll close the loop."
- Live results per region, size band, seniority and offer are tracked weekly by the machine's Coach and on the Outbound Machine dashboard (Results page). Summarise durable lessons here as they land.
